What size gate opener do I need for LiftMaster LA500PKGUL (18 Ft / 1,600 Lb) Commercial Swing (18 ft, 950 lbs)?

While the gate weighs 950 lbs physically, factoring in a 18-foot lever arm (1.44x leverage) and 45% wind-sail surface area (+132 lbs at 30 MPH) requires an automatic gate opener rated for at least 1,890 lbs and 18+ feet.

What size solar panel and battery do I need for 60 gate cycles per day?

The control board's 24/7 radio receiver draws ~0.96 Ah/day in standby, plus 4.77 Ah/day for 60 open/close cycles = 5.73 Ah/day (73.3 Wh/day). Install a 50W solar panel facing south and a 12V 110Ah deep-cycle battery.

Do I need an automatic magnetic gate lock on a 18-foot swing gate?

Yes! Any swing gate 12 feet or longer acts as a giant lever arm against the opener's front mount. Installing a 2,000-lb automatic electromagnetic lock at the latching end prevents wind buffeting and stops intruders from forcing the actuator gears.
